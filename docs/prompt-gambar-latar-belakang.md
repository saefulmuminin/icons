## Paragraf 2 — Tantangan yang mengglobal

> Kemiskinan transnasional, krisis kemanusiaan, konflik, bencana, dan perubahan
> iklim yang tak bisa diselesaikan satu negara sendirian.

```
Photorealistic composite collage, seamless feathered blends between scenes,
warm late-afternoon light, subtle green accent tones.
Scenes: relief tents in a dry aid camp at golden hour; aid workers in green
vests unloading sacks of rice from a truck; a family wading through receding
flood water carrying water containers; cracked drought earth with a farmer
looking up at the sky; children of different ethnicities — Southeast Asian,
South Asian, African, Middle Eastern — holding aid parcels and smiling gently.
Centre motif: a softly glowing globe with thin arcs of light linking the
continents, hands cupped beneath it.
Mood: dignified, hopeful, never distressing or pitying. Faces calm and
composed, not crying.
No text, no readable signage, no organisation logos, no recognisable national
flags, no depiction of any identifiable real conflict.
941x1672 portrait orientation, high detail, natural skin tones, documentary photography look.
```

## Paragraf 3 — Mengapa tema ini

> Transformasi dari instrumen berdampak lokal menjadi kekuatan kolaboratif
> global: inovasi pengelolaan, tata kelola kuat, penyelarasan nilai kemanusiaan
> antarnegara.

```
Photorealistic composite collage, seamless feathered blends between scenes,
clean daylight office tones with green accents.
Scenes: a modern zakat operations room, staff at desks watching large wall
screens filled with abstract charts and a world map; an Indonesian woman in a
hijab presenting confidently beside a screen of data visualisations; two
officials in batik shaking hands over a signed document at a formal table; a
field officer in a green vest with a tablet standing beside a small rural
house, verifying a recipient family.
Centre motif: a luminous globe with arcs of light radiating from Indonesia to
other continents, threads converging into one point of light.
Mood: competent, modern, institutional but warm.
Screens must show only abstract graphs, maps and dashboards — no readable text
anywhere in the image. No logos, no brand names.
941x1672 portrait orientation, high detail, natural skin tones, corporate documentary photography.
```

## Paragraf 4 — Menuju kolaborasi

> Kolaborasi lembaga zakat, filantropi, pemerintah, akademisi, dan swasta di
> tingkat internasional demi pembangunan manusia dan kesejahteraan berkelanjutan.

```
Photorealistic composite collage, seamless feathered blends between scenes,
warm optimistic daylight, green accent tones.
Scenes: an international forum, delegates of many nationalities in formal
attire and batik seated around a round table in conversation; a firm handshake
between two delegates; university researchers and a professor reviewing a
printed paper together; a green city skyline with rooftop solar panels and
tree-lined streets; a smiling small-business owner arranging stock in a tidy
shop; a young graduate in cap and gown.
Centre motif: many hands of different skin tones cupped together around a young
seedling in soil, soft light glowing from the centre.
Mood: shared purpose, prosperity, forward-looking.
No text, no readable signage, no organisation logos, no national flags.
941x1672 portrait orientation, high detail, natural skin tones, documentary photography look.
```
